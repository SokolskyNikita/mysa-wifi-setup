Mysa Wi-Fi setup

Connect a Mysa smart thermostat to Wi-Fi over Bluetooth, straight from your browser, without the Mysa app or a Mysa account. Your Wi-Fi password goes only to the thermostat; this page can't send anything over the internet.

Before you start

  • The thermostat's screen shows the Bluetooth icon (setup mode). If it doesn't, hold both arrow buttons until the 10-second countdown ends and it restarts. If it shows a Wi-Fi icon instead, this page can't set it up.
  • Supported: Mysa Baseboard Lite (tested), Mysa Baseboard V1 and V2, Mysa for In-Floor Heating and Mysa for AC.
  • Coming soon: Mysa Baseboard V3, Mysa for Central HVAC and the Mysa Climate Sensor. Besides Wi-Fi, they need a credential file from Mysa's cloud that this page can't get yet. Use the Mysa app for those for now.
  • Use a 2.4 GHz network with WPA2 or mixed WPA2/WPA3 security. Mysa can't join 5 GHz-only or WPA3-only networks.
  • Use Chrome, Edge or Opera on a Mac, Windows PC, Chromebook or Android phone, close to the thermostat.

3. Pair

Click Pair. The thermostat will then show a code on its screen, then try to pair with your phone/laptop. If the codes match, click Pair/Accept on your device. Sometimes takes 2-3 tries.

4. Wi-Fi

After pairing, ask the thermostat to list the Wi-Fi networks it can see.

Green: good (−70 dBm or stronger). Amber: weak (−71 to −79 dBm). Red: poor (−80 dBm or weaker). Signal quality is an estimate; interference can still affect reliability.
